Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 4, 2026

Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains what data the Inevitable Bot ("the Bot", "we", "us", or "our") collects, why we collect it, and how it is used when you interact with the Bot on Discord.

1. Data We Collect

To provide its core functionality, the Bot may collect and store the following information:

  • Guild/Server Information: Server IDs and configured channel IDs (such as moderation log channels).
  • User Information: User IDs are processed to execute commands, apply moderation actions, and manage permissions.
  • Message Content: The Bot temporarily processes message content when commanded (e.g., executing a command or scanning for specific keywords like "inevitable"). We do not permanently store your message history unless explicitly required for a feature you enable (e.g., logging a specific moderation action to your server's log channel).

2. How We Use Your Data

We use the collected data exclusively to:

  • Execute the commands you request.
  • Provide server-specific configurations (such as sending logs to the correct channel).
  • Maintain and improve the stability and performance of the Bot.

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or tracking across platforms.

3. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties. Your data is only stored in our secure database (MongoDB) hosted on verified infrastructure, and is accessible only to the core development team for maintenance purposes.

4. Data Retention and Deletion

Data linked to your server (such as settings and log channels) is retained as long as the Bot remains in your server and the settings are active. Server administrators can remove their data at any time by using the appropriate settings commands (e.g., /removelog) or by kicking the Bot from the server, which will cease further data collection.

5. Security

We implement commercially reasonable security measures to protect your data against unauthorized access, alteration, or destruction. However, please be aware that no method of transmission over the internet or method of electronic storage is 100% secure.

6. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes in our practices or features. We encourage you to review this page occasionally. Continued use of the Bot implies your consent to the updated policy.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy or your data, please reach out to us via our Support Server.
